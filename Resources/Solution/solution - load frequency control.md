### Load Frequency Control (LFC) Example

![[ee_2014(2)#^q51]]

---
#### Solution (step-by-step)

In steady-state synchronous operation, **there is only one system frequency**

##### Step 1 : Basic formula
$$f = f_{1} - R_{1}P_{1} = f_{2} - R_{2}P_{2}$$
$R_{i}$ : Speed Regulation Constant for Generator $i$
$P_{i}$ : Power Contributed by Generator $i$

##### Step 2 : Finding Power Relations

$$\therefore \quad \quad \quad 51.5 - (1)\cdot P_{1} = 51 - (1)\cdot P_{2}$$
$$P_{1} - P_{2} = 0.5$$
And given $P_{1}$ and $P_{2}$ share $2.5 \text{ MW}$ 
$$\therefore P_{1} + P_{2} = 2.5$$
We get $P_{1} = 1.5$ and $P_{2} = 1$

##### Step 3 : Calculating Steady-State Frequency
$$f = 51.5 - (1)\cdot 1.5 = 50 \text{ Hz}$$
---
##### Answer

Correct numerical value is **50**
