#type-A-chopper 

> The power flow in type-A chopper is always from source to load.
> $V_o \lt V_s$ : **Step-down chopper**

![[Pasted image 20250823191044.png]]

1. $CH$ is **on** : $V_o = V_s$ ; current $i_o$ flows in the arrow direction
2. $CH$ is **off** : $V_o = 0$ ; current $i_o$ flows in the same direction as previous through *freewheeling diode* $FD$
3. So, average voltage $(V_o)$ and average current $(I_o)$ are always positive.
