> **Thyristors** is derived from **THYR**atron and trans**ISTORS** → Thyristors is a solid state device like a [[Power Transistors|transistor]] (by construction) and has characteristics similar to that of a thyratron tube.

**Thyristors** is a *family name* of semiconductors for
1. Thyristor or SCR (Silicon-Controlled Rectifier) (most widely used) (thyristor is synonymous with SCR)
2. Triac
3. Diac
4. Silicon-controlled switches
5. Programmable Unijunction Transistor (PUT)
6. GTO
7. RCT
